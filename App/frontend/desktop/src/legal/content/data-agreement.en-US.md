# Cuby Data Agreement

(Data Use and Privacy Policy)

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Effective: November 23, 2026

This Data Agreement explains how the Cuby desktop client ("Cuby") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects your data. It is issued by Suanova Technology Ltd ("we", "us" or "our") and applies to Cuby and related services. Please read it carefully before use, especially the provisions on cross-border processing, retention periods, and your rights.

## 1. Overview and Principles

1.1 We process your data in accordance with the principles of local-first processing, data minimization, and user control.

1.2 Local first: where possible, your conversations, memory, and configuration data are stored on your device and managed directly by you.

1.3 Data minimization: we collect only the data necessary for the purposes described in this Agreement and avoid using it beyond those purposes.

1.4 User control: you may manage memory, disable the Improvement Program, delete local data, or request account cancellation as described in Section 7.

## 2. Where Your Data Is Stored

2.1 Your conversation records, memory data, configuration information, and access credentials are stored locally on your device by default.

2.2 Your CubeRouter Platform Account information (including account identifiers, authentication information, and quota records) is stored and managed by the CubeRouter platform. CubeRouter is operated by us, and its account data is subject to CubeRouter platform rules.

2.3 Cloud service providers engaged by us support basic operational analytics, update checks, and voice transcription in account mode. Relevant data may be stored or processed in the locations where we or those providers operate.

2.4 Data storage locations may evolve with service versions and deployment methods. We will handle such changes in accordance with this Agreement and applicable law.

## 3. Information We Collect

3.1 CubeRouter account information: account identifiers, sign-in credentials, and quota and billing records.

3.2 Device and log information: device type, operating system version, application version, operational logs, and error information, used to maintain the Service and troubleshoot issues.

3.3 Credentials stored locally: access credentials required to connect to CubeRouter and model services, stored on your local device.

3.4 Memory data: information formed through Memory Collection after you authorize it.

3.5 Voice input: when you use voice input, the relevant audio is processed and transcribed into text.

3.6 Basic operational analytics events: feature usage, page visits, and performance metrics used to understand how the Service operates.

3.7 Improvement Program data: if you enable the Improvement Program, we may collect and store conversations, prompts, agent operation records, memory data, feature usage behavior, and crash and error information.

## 4. When Data Leaves Your Device

4.1 Sign-in and registration: when you sign in with a Platform Account, authentication information is sent to CubeRouter.

4.2 Memory extraction: when you enable model-based memory extraction, relevant content is sent through CubeRouter to Model Services for processing.

4.3 Model requests: requests you send to agents or models are routed through CubeRouter to model providers for processing, and related request and response data passes through those systems.

4.4 Voice transcription: when you use voice input, audio is sent to the voice transcription service engaged by us for processing.

4.5 Analytics events and update checks: basic operational analytics events and version update checks are sent to cloud service providers engaged by us.

4.6 Line selection: Cuby offers a mainland China line and a Hong Kong line. If you select the Hong Kong line, your requests and related data may be processed by servers in Hong Kong under the data handling arrangements applicable to that line. Please choose the line according to your needs.

4.7 Improvement Program: if you enable the Improvement Program, the data described in Section 3.7 is transmitted and processed for the purposes of the Improvement Program.

## 5. CubeRouter, Cloud Providers, and Third-Party Model Providers

5.1 CubeRouter is operated by us and is responsible for platform accounts, model access routing, and billing. Account and Model Service data is processed in accordance with this Agreement and CubeRouter platform rules.

5.2 Cloud service providers engaged by us provide infrastructure, analytics, update checks, voice transcription, and other services. We require them by contract to protect your data and to process it only in accordance with our instructions.

5.3 Third-party model providers process model request data routed through CubeRouter under their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend that you review the policies of the relevant providers before use.

5.4 Third-party agents, tools, and messaging channels process relevant data independently under their own privacy policies. You should carefully select them and understand how they process data.

## 6. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure

6.1 We do not sell your personal information.

6.2 We may share necessary data with cloud service providers engaged by us and third-party model providers for the purposes described in this Agreement.

6.3 We may disclose relevant data as required by laws, regulations, or mandatory requirements of judicial or administrative authorities.

6.4 In the event of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets, we may transfer relevant data and will require the transferee to remain bound by this Agreement.

6.5 Except as expressly provided in this Agreement or required by law, we will not share your personal information with other third parties without your consent.

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

7.1 You may disable the Improvement Program at any time and withdraw your consent to the processing of Improvement Program data.

7.2 You may view, manage, export, delete, or request deletion of memory data in Cuby.

7.3 You may delete locally stored conversations, configuration, and credential data using the delete or clear controls provided in Cuby, or by manually removing the data from your device. Uninstalling the application may not delete locally stored data.

7.4 You may access and correct your account information in accordance with CubeRouter platform rules.

7.5 You may cancel your Platform Account in accordance with CubeRouter platform rules, or request cancellation by contacting us at info@isuanova.com. After cancellation, we will handle relevant data in accordance with Section 9.

7.6 If you have questions about data processing or wish to exercise other rights, you may contact us at info@isuanova.com.

## 8. Data Security

8.1 We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

8.2 Your local device, Platform Account, and access credentials are in your custody. Please use strong passwords, keep your system updated, and avoid signing in on untrusted devices.

8.3 In the event of a data security incident, we will take remedial measures and provide notifications as required by applicable law.

## 9. Retention

9.1 Local data remains on your device until you delete it using the delete or clear controls provided in Cuby, or manually remove it from your device.

9.2 Cloud data (including CubeRouter account data and data processed by cloud service providers engaged by us) is retained for the shortest period necessary to achieve the purposes described in this Agreement, unless otherwise required by law or necessary for handling disputes.

9.3 After the retention period expires, we will delete or anonymize the relevant data. We will respond to requests for deletion in accordance with applicable law.

## 10. Minors

10.1 If you are under the age of 18, you should read this Agreement with your guardian and use the relevant features only with your guardian's consent.

10.2 We recommend that guardians pay attention to minors' data management and help them understand how features such as memory, voice, and the Improvement Program process data.

10.3 If we discover that a minor has provided personal information without guardian consent, we will handle the relevant data in accordance with applicable law.

## 11. Updates, Language, and Contact

11.1 We may update this Agreement from time to time and will inform you through release notes, in-app notices, or other means. Updated terms take effect on their effective date.

11.2 This English version is provided for convenience only; if there is any conflict, the Chinese version prevails. The Chinese version of this Agreement is the authoritative version.

11.3 If you have any questions, comments, or complaints about this Agreement or data processing, please contact us at info@isuanova.com.
